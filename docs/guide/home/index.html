Home Screen

Your home base, your way

A device status card stays pinned at the top - model, connection, power - with a customizable grid of widgets below it. Add, remove and reorder cards so the Home screen shows exactly what you care about.

Make it yours

Customize in seconds

Tap the Customize Home icon (top-left) to enter edit mode:

  • Drag a card to reorder it.
  • Tap the red circle to remove a card.
  • Add opens a sheet of widgets you don't have yet.
  • Reset restores the defaults; Done exits.
Cards are dimmed while editing so you can't change a receiver setting by accident. Widgets only render when your receiver exposes the feature.
Name your rooms

"Living Room", not "MAIN ZONE"

Long-press the zone name on the Home screen to rename any zone - the main zone included. The name is written to the receiver itself, not just the app, so it shows up in the manufacturer's own app and every other client, and it works on older chassis too. Rename once, and every controller in the house agrees on what the room is called.

Sound controls

Quick toggles in Sound

  • Graphic EQ: turn the receiver's Graphic EQ on or off from Sound, Audio Features. Only available when neither Dirac Live nor Audyssey is running room correction - the app matches the receiver's own rule.
  • Subwoofer: mute or enable the subwoofer from Sound, Audio Features, without diving into Speaker setup. Only shown on receivers that report and accept it.
  • Audio Restorer: the receiver's compressed-audio restorer - Restorer on Denon, M-DAX on Marantz - as a toggle under Sound, Tone, right next to the DAC Filter. Bring the life back into MP3s and streaming sources without a trip to the on-screen menu.
Now Playing & HEOS

See and control what's playing

When the active source exposes metadata, the Now Playing card shows album art, track / artist / station info and transport (play / pause / next / previous).

To browse music - stations, queues, streaming services, HEOS rooms - tap Launch HEOS on the Now Playing card. AVR Maestro opens the official HEOS app for browsing; once playback starts there, AVR Maestro takes back over the now-playing display, transport and widgets. Analog, optical/coaxial and HDMI passthrough sources don't report metadata, so the card stays empty for those.

Common questions

Home Screen FAQ

Can I customize the Home screen?
Yes - tap Customize Home, then drag to reorder, tap the red circle to remove, Add for more widgets, Reset for defaults, Done to finish. Cards dim while editing so you can't change a setting by accident.
Can I rename my zones, including the main zone?
Yes - long-press the zone name on the Home screen. The name is written to the receiver itself, so it shows in the manufacturer's app and every other client, and it works on older chassis too.
Does it show what's playing?
Yes, for sources that expose metadata - album art, track/artist/station and transport. Tap Launch HEOS to browse; AVR Maestro takes back over the now-playing display once playback starts.
