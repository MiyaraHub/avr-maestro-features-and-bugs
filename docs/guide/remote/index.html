The remote

A real remote, plus the live picture

Full D-pad and transport control with a live header that shows the input, sound mode, and the actual channels in use - so you always know what your Denon or Marantz is doing. Four pages: swipe or tap the page tabs.

Four pages

Everything, without the physical remote

  • Main Remote: command bar (Power / Info / Setup / Option / Back), D-pad, transport, and a volume bar - with a live header showing input, sound mode and the IN/OUT channels in use.
  • Inputs: the full source grid. Rename and reorder sources to match your gear - open the source list, tap a tile to edit, tap a source to rename it, drag to reorder. Renames are written to the receiver itself, so they show immediately here, in the manufacturer's app and in every other client - older chassis included. The order saves per receiver.
  • Quick / Smart Select: tap a slot to recall the receiver's saved scene; long-press to Rename or Save current state (a wizard lets you pick exactly which settings to capture). Slot renames are saved on the receiver too, so they follow you to every other app.
  • System: direct controls - HDMI output, dimmer/illumination, triggers, ECO, auto standby, sleep timer, CEC, HDMI audio out, speaker preset, picture/video mode, all-zone stereo, auto lip sync, audio delay, input mode. Unsupported items are hidden.
Custom input icons

Your gear, your icons

Stock icons are fine - until your Shield, your HTPC and your Blu-ray player all deserve their real faces. Give any input its own icon from any image you have.

  • Long-press an input tile and pick an image - PNG, JPG, ICO, WebP and more all work.
  • Position it in a passport-style cropper: drag to move, pinch to zoom - what's inside the frame is your icon. Tap Save icon and you're done.
  • The icon shows on the input tiles and the Home dashboard - wherever that source appears.
  • Custom icons ride along in your settings backup, so they move to a new phone with the rest of your setup.
Desktop

Keyboard shortcuts on Mac & Windows

On the Mac App Store and Microsoft Store builds you can drive playback and volume straight from the keyboard - no reaching for the mouse. Use Cmd on macOS or Ctrl on Windows, and your keyboard's dedicated media keys work too. Nothing fires while you're typing in a text field, so renaming an input or entering an IP is never interrupted.

Common questions

Remote FAQ

Can I rename and reorder my inputs?
Yes - open the source list, tap a tile to edit, tap a source to rename it, drag to reorder. Rename them to "Nvidia Shield", "Xbox", "PS5" - the new name is written to the receiver itself, so it shows immediately in AVR Maestro, in the manufacturer's app and in every other client, older chassis included. The order saves per receiver everywhere.
Can I give my inputs custom icons?
Yes - long-press an input tile, pick any image (PNG, JPG, ICO, WebP and more), position it in the passport-style cropper and save. The icon shows on the input tiles and the Home dashboard, and it's carried inside your settings backup.
What does the virtual remote control?
A command bar (Power/Info/Setup/Option/Back), D-pad, transport and volume, with a live header showing input, sound mode and the channels in use. The System page adds direct controls (HDMI output, dimmer, triggers, ECO, sleep, CEC and more); unsupported items are hidden.
What is Quick / Smart Select?
Denon Quick Select / Marantz Smart Select are the receiver's own saved-scene slots. Tap to recall; long-press to rename or save the current state with a capture wizard.
Does AVR Maestro have keyboard shortcuts on desktop?
Yes - on Mac and Windows you can drive transport and volume from the keyboard. Use Cmd on macOS or Ctrl on Windows: Space for Play/Pause, . (period) for Stop, Left/Right for previous/next track, Up/Down for volume, and M to mute. Your dedicated media keys (play, pause, next, previous, stop, volume, mute) work too. Shortcuts pause while you're typing in a text field.
