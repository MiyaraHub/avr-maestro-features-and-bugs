Common questions

Marantz Cinema 50 + AVR Maestro

Is there an app to control the Marantz Cinema 50?
Yes - AVR Maestro is a premium third-party controller for the Cinema 50. Over your network it controls surround modes, Audyssey MultEQ XT32 and Dirac Live, speaker presets, levels, multi-zone, the tuner and one-tap Scenes.
Does AVR Maestro control Dirac Live on the Cinema 50?
Yes. On a Marantz Cinema 50 with a Dirac Live license, AVR Maestro lists your Dirac filter slots by name and switches the active one. With a standard Audyssey preset it controls MultEQ, Dynamic EQ, Dynamic Volume and LFC instead. See the Audyssey & Dirac guide.
Do I need a cloud account?
No - it talks to the Cinema 50 directly over your local network. No cloud, no subscription, no extra hardware; nothing leaves your network.
How do I connect it?
Enable Network Control on the Cinema 50, grab its IP, and add it in the app. Full steps on the getting started guide.
