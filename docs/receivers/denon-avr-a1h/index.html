Common questions

Denon AVR-A1H + AVR Maestro

Is there an app to control the Denon AVR-A1H?
Yes - AVR Maestro is a premium third-party controller for the A1H. Over your network it controls surround modes, Audyssey and Dirac Live, speaker presets, channel levels, multi-zone, the tuner and one-tap Scenes.
Does it handle all the channels and zones?
Yes. It reads the A1H's full capability set on connect and surfaces per-channel levels, layout, presets and independent multi-zone control.
Do I need a cloud account?
No - it talks to the A1H directly over your local network. No cloud, no subscription, no extra hardware; nothing leaves your network.
How do I connect it?
Enable Network Control on the A1H, grab its IP, and add it in the app. Full steps on the getting started guide.
